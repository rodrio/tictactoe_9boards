📖 9-Boards Tic-Tac-Toe Rules

🎯 The Big Picture

This is like regular tic-tac-toe, but with a twist! Instead of one board, you have NINE boards arranged in a 3x3 grid. Your goal is to win the BIG game by winning three of the small boards in a row.

🎮 How to Play

  • Your turn: Click any empty square in any of the 9 small boards
  • Mark your territory: Place your X or O in the chosen square
  • Win a small board: Get three in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) in any individual board
  • Claim the big square: When you win a small board, that board's position in the main 3x3 grid gets marked with your symbol
  • Win the game: Get three of your symbols in a row on the main board!

🏆 Winning Conditions

To win the game: You must win 3 small boards that form a line on the main board.

  • Win the top-left, top-center, and top-right boards (horizontal win)
  • Win the top-left, center, and bottom-left boards (vertical win)
  • Win the top-left, center, and bottom-right boards (diagonal win)
  • And so on... just like regular tic-tac-toe!

🤝 What About Draws?

  • Small board draw: If a small board fills up with no winner, it gets marked with 'D' on the main board
  • Big game draw: If the entire main board fills up with no three-in-a-row winner, the whole game is a draw

💡 Strategy Tips

Think ahead! Don't just focus on winning one board. Think about which boards will help you win the main game.
Block your opponent! If you see your opponent about to win three boards in a row, try to win one of those boards first.
Control the center! The center board is part of 4 different winning lines (horizontal, vertical, and both diagonals).

🎨 Visual Guide

Green boards: Won by Player X

Red boards: Won by Player O

Yellow boards: Ended in a draw

White boards: Still in progress

❓ Quick FAQ

Q: Can I play in any board?
A: Yes! You can play in any empty square in any board on your turn.

Q: What happens when a board is won?
A: That board closes and its position gets marked on the main board. You can't play in it anymore.

Q: How long does a game take?
A: Usually 10-20 minutes, depending on how strategic you are!
